## Product Purpose
An architecture portfolio introducing R.W.H Group's projects and connecting visitors to its contact form and company profile.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript in `dist/`, generated by `render-projects.py`; Vercel serves the site and its existing contact API.
- Eleven existing project slides remain in their current order.
- Office opens from its existing menu item inside the landing content panel, keeping the landing URL. `/office` is the source/fallback document, like existing project detail documents.
- Reuse the existing Vietnamese/English controls, shared navigation, footer, contact form, company PDF and video library.

## Brand Commitments
Keep the incumbent R.W.H UI, fonts and neutral text colors. Office now follows the complete content sequence and image relationships of the parent R.W.H Office page. The user's later request replaces the earlier brief for a short Apollo-style statement.

## Evidence on Hand
- `PROJECT_SOURCES.json` and `VIDEO_SOURCES.json` own the existing portfolio.
- `OFFICE_CONTENT.json` records the layout and factual content sources for the new page.
- Office includes leadership, teams, portrait, map, biography, philosophy, signature, Japan/Vietnam services and partner logos. Page text switches between Vietnamese and English; Japanese duplicate text is translated, while original marks inside supplied images remain intact.

## Office typography and partner motion refinement
- Keep all parent Office information and current Roboto/neutral UI. Split prose by meaning, retain full names where practical, emphasize key people/qualifications/philosophy and career years, and present each service on its own line in VI/EN.
- Both complete partner panoramas automatically move left in seamless duplicate loops. Hover/focus/touch pauses, offscreen/hidden-page pauses and a static horizontally scrollable reduced-motion fallback are retained. The user explicitly removed the visible pause/resume control.

## Office mobile presentation
- At phone widths, prioritize a compact header and leadership/portrait composition, two-column team links,16px prose, left-aligned services and proportionate moving partner strips. Preserve complete VI/EN content and all shared functionality.

## Office desktop text width
- At desktop widths ≥1001px, use a centered 900px editorial block with 16px prose, compact biography/year rows, and bold 700-weight section/subsection headings. The latest supplied reference supersedes the earlier full-grid stretch. Keep the current Roboto family/neutral colors and VI/EN content; remove the visible partner pause/resume line. Introductory sentences share one paragraph and qualification/date share one line when space allows.

## Landing content and return behavior
- Office and project details open in the existing landing panel without navigating the landing URL. Keep the original project X control; the return arrow applies only to Office and Contact.
- Contact opens above the current context and its return arrow replaces the contact menu action. Closing Contact preserves the underlying Office/project scroll position and draft form values.
- Browser Back closes Contact or an embedded native image/video dialog first, then Office/project content. The separate root video-library flow is outside this change. Reuse same-URL history entries and replace internal document navigation so it does not add spurious Back steps.
- Pause landing autoplay while Contact or a content panel is open. Resume a complete viewing interval after closing, preserving the current project. Keep API delivery, factual content, VI/EN, media and incumbent styling.
